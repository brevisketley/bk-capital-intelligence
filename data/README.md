# Research data archive

`data/snapshots/` is populated automatically every six hours by the dedicated collection workflow.

Each snapshot is a point-in-time observation of the top 100 opportunities by TVL from the DeFiLlama discovery feed. Snapshots are inputs to research and walk-forward replay; they are not investment recommendations and do not represent realized returns.

The archive is intentionally bounded. Protocol-level historical enrichment and direct on-chain validation remain separate layers.
